# State-conservation checklist

- List the physical molecules entering the transition.
- Identify which strands and segments remain, are copied, are removed, or are
  newly synthesized.
- Explain each new covalent bond, strand break, nick, gap, overhang, or topology
  change.
- Confirm that sequence does not appear without template copying, ligation, or
  another target-supported physical event.
- Confirm that cleanup and selection do not alter molecular architecture unless
  the source specifies such a change.
- Preserve distinct bound/unbound or selected/nonselected products only when
  they matter downstream; classify noncarried products as discarded.
- Ensure every noninitial substrate is the carried product of an earlier
  transition.
